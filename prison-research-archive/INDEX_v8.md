## Research-integrity rules
Only add new versioned files and categorized exact copies. Preserve publisher editions, source row/column/page references, units, fiscal and publication years, identities of source and issuer, and explicit uncertainty. The end goal remains **thorough searchable archival cataloguing with verifiable original provenance**, not a prematurely joined observational panel.
