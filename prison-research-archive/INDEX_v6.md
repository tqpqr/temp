# Research archive — working index v6 (14 October 2026)

This archive preserves originals without editing, removing, renaming or merging them. The goal is a scientific inventory of original file contents, source agency and verifiable upstream URL, units, denominator, observation date, statistical population and exact page/sheet/cell addresses. Reading or preserving source files does not automatically make their observations longitudinally comparable.

Annual average prisoner count used for cost/per-prisoner is not the same as month-end population. Certified Normal Accommodation is not necessarily equivalent to operating capacity. Public and privately managed prisons do not have identical accounting boundaries. Direct cost changes across the 2018 restatement are a source-reporting allocation change, not demonstrated economic shocks.
