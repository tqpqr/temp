# Verified provenance: JEET exports (US)

Provenance identification (2026-10-14): the files under `jeet/2016` through `jeet/2021` match the official **Justice Expenditure and Employment Tool (JEET)** of the **U.S. Bureau of Justice Statistics (BJS)**. This is a source attribution based on the distinctive worksheet metric names and years, supported by BJS official descriptions, not a recovered browser download log.

Official access and definitions:
- https://bjs.ojp.gov/data/data-analysis-tools
- https://bjs.ojp.gov/jeet-resources
- https://bjs.ojp.gov/data-collection/justice-expenditure-and-employment-extracts-series
- https://www.ojp.gov/library/publications/justice-expenditure-and-employment-tool-jeet

Underlying federal statistical sources:
1. U.S. Census Bureau Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances (expenditure).
2. U.S. Census Bureau Annual Survey of Public Employment and Payroll (employment and payroll).

Distinctive JEET metrics seen in verified spreadsheets:
- Expenditures Per Capita
- Employees Per 10k Residents
- Average Pay
Function: Corrections. Geographic rows: 50 US states plus DC. Source files organized into folder years 2016–2021. This matches the year span currently described by BJS JEET.

Methodology warnings:
- Corrections includes institutional and certain noninstitutional corrections expenditure and employment (e.g. probation and parole). Do not equate with prison operating costs alone.
- Per-capita expenditure denominator is resident population, not prison inmate population.
- Average payroll uses March payroll divided by number of employees, per BJS definitions.
- BJS online documentation for employment per 10,000 residents appears to contain an inconsistent sentence referring to expenditure as numerator; use the actual JEET label and confirm underlying employee figures rather than blindly copying that sentence.
- Aggregate values identical in 2016 and 2017 remain an open data quality verification item. Official provenance does not by itself resolve comparability or revisions.
- Filename years and exact file export timestamps are not externally established.

Originals must never be modified, deleted, moved or replaced. This is an additive provenance record.
