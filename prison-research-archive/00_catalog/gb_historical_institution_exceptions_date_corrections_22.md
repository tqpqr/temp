# Historical exceptions: dated correction and unresolved annex issues (pass 22)

This add-only note qualifies earlier generated event and historical-status CSVs; none were overwritten.

## High Down: date resolved to April 2022
The 2021/22 Annex A calls High Down `Male Local`; the 2022/23 Annex A **already calls it Male Category C**, although the 2023/24 appendix adds footnote 17 saying it changed from Male Local to Category C. The footnote is therefore not reliable evidence for a *2023/24 change date*. Independent official inspection (HMI Prisons 2023) and Independent Monitoring Board 2023 annual report establish completion **April 2022**.

## Historical aliases are not independent operational prisons
Annexes include records of past closed/merged/renamed units alongside active constituents. A historical `Usk / Prescoed` row appears alongside the separate `Usk` and `Prescoed` rows in FY2021/22 and FY2022/23. FY2024/25 separates constituent entries, whereas the workforce still uses `Usk/Prescoed`. Likewise `Morton Hall (IRC)` may coexist with `Morton Hall` and `The Verne (IRC)` with `The Verne` in older annexes. **Never count listed annex rows as separate contemporaneous facilities without verifying status.**

## Onley/Rainsbrook: planned annex, operation not verified
Ministry of Justice press release dated **2 August 2023** announced a plan to convert former Rainsbrook Secure Training Centre into a Category C annex of HMP Onley (up to 131 places), with proposed completion early 2025. This is an **announced plan**, NOT evidence that the annex became operational or that the workforce row added staffing. At the 2025 HMP Onley inspection, the inspected prison is listed with operational capacity 742 and the report does not establish the former Rainsbrook annex had opened. Thus Onley remains **provisionally in the 93-site sensitivity panel**, flagged for site-capacity reconciliation before causal work.

Official evidence:

- https://cdn.websitebuilder.service.justice.gov.uk/uploads/sites/19/2025/07/Onley-2025-web.pdf

## Dartmoor: closure versus FY functional category
FY2024/25 registry still calls Dartmoor Category C, but government responses state all prisoners were decanted by July 2024, with temporary closure from August, and no reopening decision by July 2025. The source function category alone cannot prove operating activity or staffing-demand comparability.
