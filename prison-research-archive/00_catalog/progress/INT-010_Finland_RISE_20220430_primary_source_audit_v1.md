## Exact 30-Apr-2022 custodial accounting
- Table **1-1 physical PDF p8**: total correctional clients **6121** = VV prison sentence **2362** + SV fine-default prison **101** + TV remand **657** = **3120 imprisoned**; plus community YKP1195 VALRA54 AEV27 ERN695 NR12 EAV1018 = **3001** non-prison correctional clients. Grand total 3120+3001=6121; unit and status definitions distinguished (MB051).
- Table **2-17 physical PDF p16** prison legal-status categories vv2093 + vv+tv60 + elink189 + yr20 + tv657 + sv94 + sv+tv7 = **3120**, reconciling Table1-1 VV 2362 =2093+60+189+20, SV 101 =94+7. `Vankeusvangeista valvotussa koevapaudessa` **259** supervised trial release is a subset of sentenced people, never additive.
- Main occupancy definition **PDF p7**: people counted as `Tiloja käytössä` (using places) exclude prisoners outside facilities for reasons **not requiring a place to be reserved**, such as supervised trial release, external institutions, custody listed at police station, unauthorised departures from open facility and unreachable persons on probation. Hence registered prisoner stock **not equal** facility room occupancy numerator (MB050).
