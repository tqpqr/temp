## Definition and reference period
- PDF printed p1: nationwide *planned placement allocations* and specialist prison departments, mainly 2022–2024, with additional preliminary 2024–2026 planning, subject to new buildings and administrative implementation. Refers to Imprisonment Act chapter 15 section 16 regarding prison internal rules; it is not a measurement of current operational bed-days.
- Printed p2–4: scheduled specialist wards for remand/sentenced prisoners, women, minors/young inmates and safety-risk groups. Separate commencement dates mean no single contemporaneous capacity observation.
- Printed p5: explicitly based on forecast inmate volumes and **90% institutional occupancy target**. Nationwide allocation to be reviewed at least annually and amended when necessary. New MB-036: planned allocation is NOT realized capacity.
- Printed p7: attachments listed: 1 background, 2 "Valtakunnallinen vankipaikkataulukko" (facility allocation table), 3 implementation timetable. These annexes are NOT INCLUDED in the seven-page PDF. The facility-by-facility full allocation is therefore NOT extractable here.

## Verifiable examples, all PLANNED not observed
Printed p2: safety-related 13 wards / 147 places targeted for 1 March 2022. New-section names: Riihimäki 4 sections/60 places, Turku 3/39, Sukeva 2/22, Mikkeli 2/10. These total 11 sections/131 places; two existing security wards of 8 places each are also mentioned at Riihimäki and Turku, reconciling totals to 13/147. Do NOT interpret 147 as necessarily 147 net new places.
Printed pp2–3: women's allocation Kuopio 16 places (6 remand, 4 separated, 6 sentenced), Vaasa 10 (4,2,4), planned for 2023. At Pyhäselkä 17 women's places are planned to be removed early 2023.
Printed pp3–4: Vantaa, Helsinki, Jyväskylä and Kerava youth wards have separate start years 2022 and 2023; Vaala remand 10 places conditional on opening in 2024; Kylmäkoski expansion in 2022 and 2024.

## Comparison restrictions
Finnish 2021 RISE target allocation (90% occupancy basis, implementation in future years) is NOT Italian INT-001 31 Jan 2023 DAP regulatory capacity snapshot with a 9 m2 + 5 m2 area rule, nor UK fiscal-year Baseline CNA. None is automatically a national operational staffed capacity-year measure. This administrative planning decision cannot be used as an observed prison-year denominator or combined with British and Italian figures without concept/date reconciliation.
