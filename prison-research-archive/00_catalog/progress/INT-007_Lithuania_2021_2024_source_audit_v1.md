# INT-007 — Lithuanian Prison Service / VDA monthly inmates CSV: original-source audit
**DONE 2026-10-15**. Preserved original `space-archive/extra/stp8/LaikomuAsmenuStatistika.csv` (Git SHA1 `acd029c2f0975ec123007889e0e8cfd274d401f1`; 63,903 original bytes). Decoded original CSV UTF-8; 21 fields, 312 rows; 39 exact month-end dates **2021-01-31 through 2024-03-31**; exactly 8 source institutions each month. The 312 original physical CSV row numbers, VDA IDs and original names are retained in [full row QA](INT-007_Lithuania_312_original_row_QA_v1.csv); no malformed rows or duplicate date+literal site names.

## Exact identity, issuer and scope
Official title **Kalinimo įstaigose laikomų asmenų statistika** by **Lietuvos kalėjimų tarnyba** (producer, Lithuanian Prison Service), via **Valstybės duomenų agentūra** (publisher). [Government dataset](https://data.gov.lt/datasets/2665/), [government official model with literal field definitions](https://data.gov.lt/datasets/2665/versions/1367/models/LaikomuAsmenuStatistika/), [official announcement 2024-05-23](https://vda.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/atverti-kalinimo-istaigose-laikomu-asmenu-duomenys/). Publisher dataset created 2024-05-22 and later updated beyond the archive end date; **archived file itself ends March 2024**. Archived download/vintage date UNKNOWN; **no publisher-versus-archived CSV byte identity comparison was performed**, only official schema/issuer matching. No agency contact.

The actual observational grain is **persons at facility at month end**, not annual average. **14 original count variables**: 9 complete for 312 source rows; 5 new subcategories present **only in Jan-Mar2024 (24 rows each)**, earlier **288 cells each blank, NOT zero**. [Field dictionary](INT-007_Lithuania_14_field_dictionary_v1.csv). **No place capacity, bed count, occupancy, expenditure or staff indicator**. There are **16 distinct literal institution labels** across 2021–2024, reflecting a 2023 break. Sums of eight source rows are researcher-derived and must NOT be described as a separately publisher-certified national total. [All 39 eight-site sums](INT-007_Lithuania_39_monthly_site_sums_v1.csv): 5392 persons Jan2021; 5020 Jan2022; 5032 Mar-Dec2022; 5040 Jan2023; 4553 Dec2023; 4572 Mar2024.

## Confirmed methodological breaks
**MB-041 — 2022 exact repeated month profiles:** February 2022 matches January for all 8 institutions and 14 original numeric columns; April–December 2022 each equals March, same fields and names (10 consecutive identical dated month-pairs). Different record IDs/dates do not validate independent measurements; **cause of repeats is unknown**. Preserve all source rows and mark, don't invent genuine stasis or issuer mistake.
**MB-042 — January 2023 reporting-unit identity break:** 8 site-name strings in Dec2022 have **zero literal overlaps** with Jan2023. Pre-2023 standalone `Laisvės atėmimo vietų ligoninė` hospital row disappears as a named unit; old `Pravieniškių PN-AK` single label becomes first/second Pravieniškės prison labels. Neither causal restructuring detail nor exact one-to-one mapping is established from the original source. Don't autojoin same-position sites.
**MB-043 — January 2024 imprisonment/arrest accounting change:** Source `asm_sk` for **288/288 pre-2024 rows** = `suimtieji_sk + nut_laisves_atemimu_sk + nut_arestu_sk`; for **24/24 Jan-Mar2024 rows** = `suimtieji_sk + nut_laisves_atemimu_sk`. In the 24 new rows, `nut_laisves_atemimu_sk` exactly equals **six nested categories including `nut_arestu_sk`** (24/24). Arrest cannot be added twice in 2024; 2023/24 raw prison-sentence series are definition-incompatible without explicit bridge.

**Other QA:** 312/312 foreign citizen decompositions `uzs_pil_sk=suim_uzs_pil_sk+nut_uzs_pil_sk`. Women, minors and foreign citizens are subsets of all inmates, not additional separate people. All 39 dates are month ends, including leap 2024-02-29. Original ID gaps are NOT prison missingness evidence.
