## Page-level methodological evidence (printed pages 1–6)
- **PDF p2 (zero-based index 1), Definitions**: cost per place = direct/overall resource expenditure ÷ **Baseline CNA**, while cost per prisoner = direct/overall expense ÷ **average prisoner population**. Baseline CNA excludes certain routine non-residential cells but is **NOT reduced for places temporarily unavailable** (e.g. damaged cells/building work). Baseline CNA is therefore *not the same construct as operationally available place-days*. This is material to vacancy/downsizing analyses.
- **PDF p2, Definitions**: annual average prisoner population is the **mean of 12 month-end populations**, not a daily person-days denominator or point-in-time population. Do not equate automatically with annual-average daily occupancy from another statistical series.
- **PDF p2, Definitions**: direct = net expenditures managed and recorded at prisons; overall = direct + net expenditure at regional/national level recorded in NOMS annual accounts. PFI and Operate & Maintain are distinct contract models.
- **PDF p3 (zero-based 2), Table 1**: FY2015/16 CNA **78,271**; average pop **84,705**; direct **£1,897,972,242**; overall **£2,753,747,261**; direct per CNA/prisoner **£24,249 / £22,407**; overall per CNA/prisoner **£35,182 / £32,510**. These are original PDF values; do not duplicate supplementary register cells if already represented.
- **PDF p4 (zero-based 3), Table 2a & notes**: 2014/15 comparative figures **restated** because of moving maintenance expenditures to HQ following the new facilities-management contract in June 2015 (−£100,192,207 from *2014/15 direct*), and transferring finance staff costs to HQ (−£901,250 from *2014/15 direct*). Combined direct adjustment **−£101,093,457**; restated 2014/15 direct **£1,908,304,522** vs original **£2,009,397,979**. Overall total **£2,804,394,939** unchanged in that comparative column. This is an accounting reallocation, **not observed real savings**.
- **PDF p4, Table 2a**: inflation comparison uses GDP deflator **100 / 99.561** (ONS 30 September 2016); nominal FY2014/15 restated direct and GDP-deflated prior-year amounts must not be mixed in a homogeneous nominal panel.
- **PDF p4–5 (zero-based 3–4), Table 2b & note 2**: direct/overall public and contracted prison per-unit financial costs explicitly **not directly comparable** because of accounting and inclusion differences. PFI includes interest; many private establishments include health and education costs.
- **PDF p5 (zero-based 4)**: no change inferred from printed labels `Operate & Manage` vs definition `Operate & Maintain` without independent group crosswalk; classification wording must be preserved as printed.
- **PDF p6 (zero-based 5)**: administrative IT data subject to inherent recording errors. No numerical validation implied.
- **PDF p3–5**: expenditure and averages rounded to integers, totals calculated using unrounded underlying values; check before treating displayed integer division deviations as errors.
