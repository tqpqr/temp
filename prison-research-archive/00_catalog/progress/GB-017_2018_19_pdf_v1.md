## Critical newly verified MB-032: wrong-year prior sector block
Comparing original **2017/18 PDF Table 2b p3-4** with **2018/19 PDF Table 2b p3-4**, all **12/12** previous-year sector comparator cells in the latter (CNA, average prisoners, direct GBP, overall GBP × public/PFI/Operate & Manage) **exactly match the FY2016/17 values** printed in the original 2017/18 report, not the actual FY2017/18 sector values. Specific public-sector evidence:

| Measure | Actual FY2017/18 (Oct 2018 PDF) | Claimed FY2017/18 comparator (Oct 2019 PDF) | True FY2016/17 (Oct 2018 PDF) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CNA | 64,096 | 65,035 | 65,035 |
| Average population | 68,448 | 69,040 | 69,040 |
| Direct GBP | 1,580,362,483 | 1,485,326,105 | 1,485,326,105 |
| Overall GBP | 2,644,577,101 | 2,467,926,949 | 2,467,926,949 |

PFI and Operate & Manage likewise exact FY2016/17 repetitions; 12-cell crosswalk `progress/GB-017_sector_comparator_QA_v2.csv` holds both editions and effect on implied adjacent-year changes.

**Strong internal crosscheck:** summing all three *claimed FY2017/18* sector prior rows in 2018/19 PDF yields 78,800 CNA / 84,750 prisoners / GBP 1,943,546,987 direct (the national FY2016/17 numbers), while 2018/19 PDF Table 2a correctly identifies FY2017/18 as 77,865 / 84,710 / GBP 2,045,831,827 direct. The table contradicts itself. This cannot be an ordinary GBP rounding difference and is not explained by a GDP deflator. Editorial carry-forward is a plausible explanation, not confirmed production history.

Therefore **DO NOT USE** 2018/19 Table 2b printed sectoral growth/change percentages for contiguous FY2017/18→FY2018/19 trends. Example public sector original direct FY2017/18 GBP 1,580,362,483 and actual FY2018/19 GBP 1,684,759,082 implies **GBP 104,396,599** nominal growth, *not* publisher Table 2b printed GBP 199,432,977 (which uses FY2016/17 as denominator). Treat real-sector comparisons as pending sector- and accounting-scope harmonisation. Preserve original published cells unchanged and register **MB-032**.
