- The 7,426 FY2022/23 prison mean ≠ 7,948 on 2023-11-13; p15 Exhibit4 shades forecast **after Dec15 2023** and 22 Dec 2023 7,932, 29 Mar 2024 8,166 are **forecasts**, NOT realised observations.
