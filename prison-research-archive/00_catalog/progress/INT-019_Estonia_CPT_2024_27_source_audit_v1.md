# INT-019 — Estonia CPT/Inf (2024) 27: verified archived **government response**, source and methodological audit

Audited 2026-10-15. **The source is NOT the CPT's own inspection report**. `CPT/Inf (2024) 27` is the *Response of the Estonian Government to the report of the European Committee for the Prevention of Torture and Inhuman or Degrading Treatment or Punishment (CPT) on its visit to Estonia from 29 May to 8 June 2023*. The **separate underlying independent CPT report** is `CPT/Inf (2024) 26`; the original 27 document quotes parts of that report, then adds explicit Estonian-government replies and later-date figures. Released in **Strasbourg 26 September 2024**. This answer is NOT an Estonian national prison annual report, and 2024 is a **publication vintage**, NOT an observation year. [Original Council of Europe public PDF](https://rm.coe.int/1680b13704) and [country visit/publication index](https://www.coe.int/en/web/cpt/estonia).

## Data catalogue — exact original text/attribution
[33 individual dated/scope-author/source-attributed observations](INT-019_Estonia_CPT_government_2023_2024_dated_indicator_observations_v1.csv). Each field has original PDF **physical page**, original source measure and its *speaker* (`CPT_QUOTED_IN_GOVT_REPLY` versus `ESTONIAN_GOVERNMENT_RESPONSE`), and a mandatory timestamp/grain/caution. Many government reply text extracts quote earlier CPT report verbatim, followed by current government response; **never conflate CPT's inspection findings with Estonian government's later corrections/replies.**
- **CPT quotation physical p12, paragraph41** (printed p12): 2023-05-29 to 2023-06-08 **closed estate official capacity2,921** places, **open237**, at-time-of-visit approximately **61% closed and79% open** capacity utilized; about **50 unoccupied open places**. These are approximate visit rates, not national yearly averages. Estonia **government's own reply on same PDF p12** says **closed3,041, open237** (source date of this specific capacity estimate NOT explicitly specified), and at the time of **preparing the response** open occupancy **86%**. Difference closed **+120 places**, unknown if because of different scope/source correction or actual capacity expansion. Treat as **unreconciled source-author/timing difference**, not sum 2921+3041, not +120 confirmed built new places, and do not calculate 2023 and 2024 actual prisoner headcount by multiplying rounded percentages.
- **CPT quotation p12 para42**: **1,854 prisoners** *at time of 2023 visit*, not 2024. CPT says historic **2,455 prisoners in CLOSED prisons at its 2017 visit, 3,389 prisoners in CLOSED prisons at 2012 visit**. Wording `total prison population` in 2023 versus earlier CLOSED subset is not a clean perfectly aligned longitudinal series; do not assert 24% /45% exact change of matched same census based solely on this publication. Source historical incarceration rates **250 per 100k in 2012;210 in 2017;162 in 2022** (last available at the visit). Rate2022 IS NOT population count2023 and no rate2024 observed here.
- **Estonian government answer physical p26 para76** precise **2024-04-18** filled prison officer FTE and authorized **posts**, prison by prison: **Tallinn 270 of 322**, **Viru 240 of 273**, **Tartu 199 of 226**. Facility totals **filled709 FTE, authorized821 posts**, gap112 nominal posts-equivalent. Do not call filled FTE headcount or full-year prison staffing average. Source published Sept2024, observation date 18April2024. Important resource rigidity measurement with explicitly aligned common date and same occupational role.
- **CPT quotations original p20 para65** *during 2023 visit* prison **healthcare professionals (different occupational perimeter!)**: Tallinn **21.9 FTE of 37.3 theoretical posts**; Tartu **31.3 of37**, Viru **22.1 of34.5**. Cross-site totals **75.3 filled FTE /108.8 theoretical**; not custody officers. Healthcare source classification can be dramatically affected by Estonian government policy in this very report p20 indicating from **1 July 2024** prison health finance/organization would transfer from prisons/Ministry of Justice to Ministry of Social Affairs, Health Insurance Fund and outside regional health providers. This is a potential **institutional/statistical series break**, not proof based on this report alone that full budget transfer occurred *on schedule* at all sites. Never infer discontinuity size.
- **Estonian reply p36 para105** 2023 site counts of broadly defined *physical assaults OR imminent threats thereof*: Tallinn **136**, Tartu **218**, Viru **128**, total482 events with broad inclusions (threat to staff, prisoner assault against others, harms), not solely violence against officers and not unique prisoners; do not call "staff assaults" or annual prison guards' victimizations. **CPT quotation p25 para75** **prison suicides 2022=3,2021=5**, event counts, not rates. **CPT p3 para6** Chancellor of Justice prison complaints **2022=344**, complaints received, NOT validated torture cases.
- **Government answer p20** healthcare financing and authority institutional reform **2024-07-01 planned/legislated** as stated in reply. Treat as *policy/institutional change described in source* rather than measured additional budget outcome or verified successful actual deployment.
- Source also covers police/border guard detention, involuntary psychiatric institutions, foreign nationals, military detention. Those numbers **MUST NOT BE MERGED** into prison-estate incarceration indicators. E.g. p36 Psychiatric Hospital 220 beds and187 patients / Ahtme Hospital75 beds and51 patients are **not prison beds or sentenced prisoners**.
