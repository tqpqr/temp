## Source definitions — PDF page 2 (zero-based p1)
- Cost per place = direct or overall resource expense divided by Baseline Certified Normal Accommodation (CNA).
- Cost per prisoner = expense divided by average prisoner population; average population = mean of twelve month-end counts, **not daily population person-days**.
- Baseline CNA is not reduced for temporarily unusable places, e.g. damaged cells/building works. Do not mistake this for average operational capacity or available place-days.
- Overall resource expense includes costs recorded regionally or nationally, while direct resources are net expenditure managed and recorded at prison sites. Legacy NOMS label in one definition is not independent proof of change in expenditure scope.
- PFI and Operate & Maintain definitions / sector cost scope reinforce existing MB-010 and MB-030. No duplicate methodology break added.
