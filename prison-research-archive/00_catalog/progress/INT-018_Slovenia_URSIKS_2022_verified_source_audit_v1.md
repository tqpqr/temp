## Reproducible original source tables
1. [Full 14 source institution / branch / youth centre occupancy rows](INT-018_14_facility_capacity_annual_population_2022_v1.csv), physical PDF p120 / publisher printed p118; 14 rows each source normative beds, calendar2022 mean prisoners, publisher occupancy %. **14/14 source occupancy calculations** (100 × year mean / source normative places) reconcile to one decimal. Capacity rows sum exactly **1,345 places**. Published average-population site rows sum **1,402.1 persons**, matching the **national rounded 1,402** (PDF p119/printed p117 and publisher press 17July2023). However **the original printed table total says 1,436.0 persons** and prints utilization **106.8%**; 1,436/1,345≈106.8%, whereas the 14 rows give **1,402.1/1,345≈104.25%**. This is a VERIFIED *source-internal grand-total error*, **33.9 people difference**, not simply harmless rounding. The printed **1,436 must be preserved as the source's erroneous literal, NOT treated as a national 2022 mean**, and the printed 106.8% is based on that same inconsistent denominator. The report's publisher paragraph a page earlier gives the correct national 1,402. The basis of the wrong total's historical provenance is unverified; do not silently "correct" the PDF. Multiple original rows represent **departments** of parent prisons; `PD Radeče` is a *juvenile reeducation* unit 47 places /12.5 annual mean, not interchangeable with regular adult sentenced prisons.
2. [Annual nine-year 2014–2022 national population/custody-budget-rate series](INT-018_2014_2022_national_mean_prisoners_cost_day_v1.csv), official PDF physical p119 / printed p117. Exact source population averages (2014..2022) **1,511;1,463;1,377;1,348;1,353;1,435;1,298;1,284;1,402** and publisher custody care-rate budget-based EUR/prisoner/day **60;62;68;80;81;90;114;104;106**. Rates are nominal, rounded, and the finance narrative defines numerator as **spent state budget funds** / calendar-year days / rounded average custody; not total across all external funding sources or cost per available place. 2021→2022 persons +9.19% (publisher says 9.2%). 2022 budget numerator EUR54,117,805.36/(365×1402)=EUR105.755/day, rounds to **EUR106**, while all-sources EUR54,718,459.56 would give EUR106.928≈107; denominator and money perimeter therefore materially affect the interpretation. The 2014–2021 rows contain published rounded rate, not exact underlying expense; **do not back-calculate exact annual budget numerators**.
