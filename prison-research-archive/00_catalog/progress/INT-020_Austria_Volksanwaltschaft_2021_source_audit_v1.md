## CRITICAL source population/universe
**This is NOT a yearly census of prisoners, prison places or prison operating costs.** It is annual National Preventive Mechanism **inspection activity** across Austrian prisons, police, youth facilities, elderly homes, disability facilities, psychiatric institutions and observation of police actions. Sources of reference in printed pages 14–17 (physical PDF pages14–17) and prison-focused chapter2.5 *Justizanstalten* printed pp121–152 (physical pages121–152). Never code prison population, country prison staff FTE, number of distinct prisons, or prison daily cost from NPM monitoring counts. Original report says repeat/return visits occur, so prison *inspections* 27 ≠ unique inspected prison institutions 27.
