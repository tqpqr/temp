# Annual Digest 2025/26 scope check

The July 2026 technical guide has no prison-function institution appendix. It provides only Appendix A on tested drug groups. Therefore a 2025/26 fiscal-year institutional function lookup cannot be generated from that guide. The 2024/25 annual source must not be silently treated as a precise June 2025 classification.

Official source: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a68d729229c578debc1a795/Guide_to_HMPPS_Annual_Digest_2025_to_2026.pdf

Earlier historical sources (FY2021/22 through FY2024/25) do contain functions/regions annexes. No source files modified.
