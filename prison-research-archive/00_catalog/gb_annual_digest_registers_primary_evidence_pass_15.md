## Primary appendix directly inspected as parsed PDF
Guide 2024/25 (42 pp), Appendix C, pdf pages 37–42 (0-based pages 36–41):
https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6889f4186478525675738fda/Guide_to_HMPPS_Annual_Digest_2024_to_2025.pdf

The appendix contains prison name, organizational region, predominant prison function, and public/private designation. It cautions that function is predominant (multi-functional sites exist), entries with blanks may refer to ceased sites, and footnotes mark status changes within year.

13 earlier unmatched institutions have been individually verified as PUBLIC and their predominant function recorded in `02_uk/gb_13_previous_unmatched_2024_25_appendix_verified.csv`.

Official guide directly distinguishes Feltham A (YCS YOI Public) and Feltham B (Category C Public), whereas workbook uses one combined name; it also records Werrington and Wetherby as YCS YOI Public and Cookham Wood as Category C Public by 2024/25. Lowdham Grange appears Category B Public. These records **do not** prove the workforce headcount has identical perimeter to Appendix C or establish earlier-year status.
