# US identification of jeet national_map workbooks

This file is an **add-only correction** to prior provisional inventory notes.

The repository owner confirmed that `jeet/` files with `exp`, `empl` and `pay` refer to **United States data**. Thus `jeet/2016` through `jeet/2021` (20 workbook files in the baseline tree) should be catalogued as a US collection rather than international/unknown by default.

Suggested new category: `US/jeet` (logical collection name, not yet a copied folder).

Provisional interpretation from filenames only:
- `exp` may refer to expenditure;
- `empl` may refer to employment;
- `pay` may refer to payroll/pay.

The precise definitions, source organization, universe, units, institutional coverage, times and worksheet columns are **unverified**. Do not equate employment with prison staff or expenditures with prison spending absent workbook evidence.

The remaining `national_map` workbook variants in `jeet/` are associated with the same US collection by directory, pending validation.

This note supersedes the earlier `national_map_annual_workbooks_unknown` topical label in the first-pass provisional files; those existing catalog files are intentionally untouched.

Baseline: Git tree `373a14b4fe725329c050ca45a8db723d304766e5`. No source files modified, moved or deleted.
