## Concrete events verified
- Aylesbury: Male Closed YOI in 2020/21 and 2021/22 → Male Category C in 2022/23 and 2023/24. YOI is not necessarily under-18.
- Brinsford: Male Closed YOI in 2022/23 → Male Category C by 2023/24, with explicit status-change note.
- Deerbolt: Male Closed YOI in 2021/22 and 2022/23 → Male Category C by 2023/24, explicit note.
- Cookham Wood: Youth Custody Estate, Male YOI - Young People 2021/22–2023/24. Changes after this must be checked with dated source.
- Lowdham Grange: private Serco in 2020/21–2022/23; private Sodexo in 2023/24 (explicit operator-change note). A change in private operator is not a transfer into directly employed HMPPS staff.
- Morton Hall: immigration removal centre in 2021/22; Male Category C prison listing 2022/23 onward. This indicates a substantial change in function, not a simple row rename.

## Crucial limitations
The evidence table contains **19 confirmed institution-financial-year cases**, deliberately NOT 105×5 exhaustive historical classification. Year of financial-year digest is Apr–Mar and does not exactly match the workforce date 30 June. The original HMPPS workforce panel is still technically complete as a staff-data extract; it is NOT yet fully institutionally audited on exact dates.

Official PDFs were text-read through the browsing interface. PDF rendered screenshots were attempted but the service returned internal errors; footnote/page layouts are therefore not visually verified. Fine-grained event timing and all remaining institutions need additional checking. **No mass automated institutional labels were invented.**

Preservation: only new crosswalk and journal files were created. No original files nor earlier generated panel files changed.
